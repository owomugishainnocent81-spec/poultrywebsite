About Golden Hen Farms

Our Goals

Provide 10,000 Ugandan families with affordable organic eggs by 2028. Train 500 smallholder farmers in sustainable poultry practices across Mukono.

Our Mission

To produce the healthiest, most ethical poultry products in Uganda while regenerating our soils and supporting local communities in Namubiru.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every family can access nutritious, cruelty-free protein from farms that heal the land and support rural livelihoods.

State of Ownership

Privately owned by Sarah Nakato. Registered with URSB. UNBS Organic Certified. NAADS approved training center since 2020.

Our Story: Founded in 2018 with 200 hens, Golden Hen now employs 14 people from Namubiru village and supplies over 40 restaurants in Kampala. Every bird lives outdoors with access to shade, dust baths, and natural forage. We rotate chickens across 6 paddocks and use solar power for our hatchery.
