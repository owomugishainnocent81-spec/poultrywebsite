History

2018

Started with 200 hens in a backyard. First eggs sold at local market in Mukono.

2020

Expanded to 1,000 birds. Got UNBS organic certification after inspection.

2022

Built new 12-acre free-range facility in Namubiru. Added hatchery unit.

2024

Reached 3,500 birds. Won “Best Organic Farm” award in Central Uganda.

2026

Launched weekly subscription boxes. Now serving 300+ families.

Welcome to Golden Hen Poultry Farm

Golden Hen is Uganda's premier free-range poultry farm located in Mukono District. We specialize in organic eggs and naturally-raised broilers for families, restaurants, and hotels across Kampala.

Our 3,500 hens roam freely on 12 acres of pasture, eating organic maize, millet, and insects. No cages, no hormones, no antibiotics — just healthy chickens producing rich, deep-orange yolk eggs.

We deliver fresh eggs every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Our broilers are processed on-site at our certified facility and delivered chilled within 6 hours of slaughter.
